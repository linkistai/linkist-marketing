## 1. Basis

This overview restates section 16 of the published Privacy Policy and adds what the product and this website show. It makes no claim the Privacy Policy does not make. The security page on this website presents the same material for a general reader.

## 2. Measures the Privacy Policy states

- Encryption in transit
- Encryption at rest where appropriate
- Access controls and role-based permissions
- Secure authentication
- One-time code expiry and secure token handling
- Audit logging
- Security monitoring
- Restricted administrative access
- Vendor security review
- Vulnerability assessment and penetration testing where appropriate
- Staff awareness and confidentiality obligations

No system is completely secure, and users are responsible for keeping their account access, email, devices and login methods secure.

## 3. Sign-in

Accounts sign in with an email address or mobile number and a one-time code that expires. There is no password. Limited security logs are kept as needed.

## 4. Logs and retention

Security and audit logs are usually kept up to 12 months and technical troubleshooting logs up to 90 days, unless needed for an investigation, security, debugging or legal defence (Privacy Policy, section 17).

## 5. Transfers

Some service providers may be outside the UAE. Safeguards where required: data processing agreements, standard contractual clauses or equivalent, confidentiality commitments, encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, vendor security assessments and data minimisation (Privacy Policy, section 15).

## 6. Incidents

A personal data breach is assessed, contained, investigated and remediated. Where the law requires, the competent authority and affected individuals are notified, with the nature of the breach, the categories of data affected, the likely consequences and the steps taken (Privacy Policy, section 21).

## 7. This website

- Static pages served with a Content Security Policy that allows scripts only from this site, Google Tag Manager and Cloudflare Turnstile, plus the headers X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy and Permissions-Policy.
- No analytics script loads before consent. No third-party script loads at all unless its key is configured.
- Forms are validated on the server, rate limited per address, protected by a honeypot and, when enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile, and deliver by email only; nothing typed into the site assistant is stored.
- Sign in and Start free hand over to the product; this website never handles credentials.
