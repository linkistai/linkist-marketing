## 1. Scope

This policy covers cookies and similar technologies (local storage, pixels, SDKs) used by this website and, as far as the published Privacy Policy states, by the Linkist app and profile pages. The Privacy Policy, section 12, sets the categories; this document lists what each category holds. Where the two differ, the Privacy Policy governs.

## 2. The categories

The Privacy Policy names three categories.

- **Essential.** Required for login, security, checkout, fraud prevention, your preferences and the core service. These cannot be switched off through the cookie notice.
- **Analytics.** Used to understand usage and diagnose problems, only where permitted and, where required, with your consent.
- **Marketing.** Used to measure campaigns or provide relevant content, only with your consent where required.

## 5. Your choices

- On this website, use the cookie notice when it appears, or the "Cookie choices" link in the footer at any time.
- In the app, use Privacy Settings, as the Privacy Policy describes.
- In your browser, block or delete cookies for linkist.ai and its subdomains. Essential functions such as sign-in may stop working.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.
