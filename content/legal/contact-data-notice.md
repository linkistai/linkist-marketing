## 1. Who this is for

You may never have used Linkist, and still be in it. Someone you met may have tapped a card, scanned your paper card, imported you from their phone or typed your details in. This notice explains what that means. It restates the published Privacy Policy for this situation and does not replace it.

## 2. Who is responsible

RatioX Labs DWC-LLC, trading as Linkist, is the data controller for data processed in Linkist. The user who added you confirmed they had the right, authority, consent or a legitimate reason to do so, and may also be responsible for the lawfulness of an import from their own device (Privacy Policy, sections 2 and 10).

## 3. What may be held about you

Depending on what the user saved: your name, email address, phone number, company, job title, notes about where and how you met, tags, follow-up reminders, interaction history, connection context, groups, and a relationship status or priority (Privacy Policy, sections 4.3 and 4.4). If the user enabled AI Enrichment, professional details may have been added from public or professional sources, and the AI features may have generated summaries, scores or suggestions about the relationship (sections 4.10 and 9).

## 4. Why it is held

Only to provide the features the user chose: organising contacts, matching against what they are looking for, reminders, relationship management and, where the user separately enabled them, the AI features (sections 6.4 and 10). Data minimisation applies to imported contacts.

## 5. What is not done with it

- It is not sold.
- You are not sent marketing because of it. Linkist does not market to imported contacts unless you opt in yourself (sections 6.4 and 10).
- It is not used to make legally binding decisions about you (section 9).

## 6. Who can see it

The user who added you. If they share the contact with their team on the Team plan, the authorised members of that team. The service providers that run Linkist, under processing terms (section 14). Authorities where the law requires.

## 7. How long it is kept

Imported data about people who are not Linkist users is kept only as long as the selected feature needs it, then deleted or anonymised under the retention schedule; AI enrichment outputs are kept for up to 6 months or until the user deletes their account (section 17). You can ask Linkist for access, correction, restriction or deletion of the data held about you, and object to processing, by writing to privacy@linkist.ai (sections 10 and 18).
