## 1. What this covers

Physical NFC cards ordered from the Linkist store, and paid subscriptions. The Terms of Service (sections 7 to 9) set the rules; this document explains them and adds the store's current practice. The Terms govern if the two differ.

## 4. Changing or cancelling an order

Cards are made to order. You may ask to change or cancel within 24 hours of placing the order; Linkist does not guarantee that a change is possible once production or fulfilment has begun. Write to support@linkist.ai with your order number.

## 5. Returns and replacements

Custom products are returnable only where:

- the product is defective
- Linkist made a production error
- the product materially differs from the confirmed order
- applicable consumer law requires a refund or replacement

Contact support@linkist.ai within 7 days of delivery for a defective or incorrect product, unless the law gives you longer. Approved refunds are usually processed within 5 to 10 business days after approval; bank and payment provider times vary.

## 6. NFC compatibility is not a defect

NFC depends on compatible devices, settings, operating system restrictions and correct use, and Linkist does not guarantee that a card works with every device. A card that works on a compatible phone is not defective because another phone has NFC switched off. Every card also carries a QR route to the same profile. ## 7. Subscriptions

Unless stated otherwise at checkout, paid subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Cancel before the renewal date in the billing hub at prm.linkist.ai to stop the next period. A period already paid for runs to its end. Linkist may change subscription features or prices with reasonable notice where the law requires it, without affecting a period already paid for unless you agree or the law requires it.
