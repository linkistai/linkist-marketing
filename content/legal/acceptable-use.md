## 1. Purpose

Linkist is for lawful, honest professional relationship management. This policy gathers the rules that the Terms of Service already set (sections 5, 13 and 14) so they can be read in one place. The Terms govern if the two differ.

## 2. What you must not do

You must not:

- use the service for illegal, fraudulent, harmful or deceptive purposes
- impersonate another person or organisation
- upload false, misleading, unauthorised or infringing content
- violate privacy, data protection, intellectual property, consumer protection, cybersecurity or communications laws
- upload or distribute malware, spyware, phishing content or harmful code
- scrape, harvest, sell or misuse personal data
- spam, mass-message or send unsolicited communications using Linkist
- attempt to gain unauthorised access to systems, accounts or data
- reverse engineer, copy, resell or misuse Linkist technology except where the law permits
- interfere with the service's security, availability or performance
- use automated tools to abuse, overload or extract data from the service
- use Linkist to profile, target, discriminate against or harm individuals unlawfully

## 3. What you must not put into Linkist

Unless expressly permitted by Linkist and lawful, you must not upload, import or process:

- government identification documents
- financial account numbers unrelated to payment processing
- health or medical records
- children's data
- biometric identifiers used for identification
- criminal records
- highly sensitive personal data
- confidential third-party information
- data obtained unlawfully

Linkist is not designed for regulated sensitive records, medical data, legal case files, financial compliance records or confidential HR records.

## 4. Your profile and the contacts you add

Anyone with your link, QR code or card can see what you publish on your profile, and you are responsible for it. When you add other people's details, you confirm you have the right, authority, consent or a legitimate reason to do so, for your own professional relationship management. The Contact Data Notice explains how their data is handled and how they can object.

## 5. AI features

AI outputs are assistive. You must not use them to make unlawful, discriminatory, harmful or unfair decisions about people, or to infer sensitive characteristics unless the law and Linkist expressly permit it.
