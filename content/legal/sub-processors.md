## 1. What a sub-processor is

A company that processes personal data on Linkist's behalf, under its instructions and under confidentiality and data processing terms (Privacy Policy, section 14). Linkist does not sell personal data.

## 3. Providers for this website

This website is separate from the app and is built so that each provider is used only when it is switched on.

| Purpose | Provider | Used when |
| --- | --- | --- |
