For many professionals, LinkedIn is the default place to build credibility online. It shows your career history, job title, company, experience, education, posts, recommendations and professional activity. It helps people discover you, understand your background and stay connected to your work.

But LinkedIn is not always the best tool for every professional introduction.

When you meet someone at a business event, client meeting, exhibition, sales conversation or networking session, you often need something faster, sharper and more focused. You need a professional identity that can be shared instantly. That is where a digital identity comes in.

::note
A LinkedIn profile helps people explore your professional background. A digital identity helps people understand who you are now, how to contact you and what action to take next.
::

The best professionals do not need to choose one over the other. They need both.

## What is a LinkedIn profile?

A LinkedIn profile is your professional presence on LinkedIn. It usually includes:

- Your name, profile photo and headline
- Current role, work experience and education
- Skills, posts and activity
- Recommendations and professional connections
- Company affiliations

It is valuable because it gives people a broader view of your career and professional reputation. A strong LinkedIn profile can help with:

- • Professional discovery
- • Hiring and recruitment
- • Thought leadership
- • Industry conversations
- • Credibility building
- • Career visibility

For many people, LinkedIn is where someone goes when they want to understand your professional background in more detail.

## What is a professional digital identity?

A professional digital identity is a focused, shareable profile that represents who you are in a specific professional context. It is not meant to replace your entire career history. It is designed to answer a more immediate question:

::note
Who are you, and how can someone connect with you right now?
::

A professional digital identity may include:

- Your name, professional headline, company and role
- Contact details, website, LinkedIn link and social links
- Services, portfolio and booking link
- Downloadable contact option
- A clear call to action
- A unique public profile link

For example, a Linkist profile may use a format such as:

::note
`m.linkist.ai/me/john-doe`
::

This profile can be shared through an NFC card, QR code, direct link, WhatsApp, email signature or social bio. The purpose is simple: make your professional identity easy to share and easy to act on.

## The key difference

A LinkedIn profile is a professional network profile. A digital identity is a professional sharing layer. That difference matters.

LinkedIn is excellent when someone wants to explore your background, see your posts, understand your career journey or connect with you inside the LinkedIn ecosystem. A digital identity is more useful when you need a clean, direct and action-oriented profile that works across situations and channels.

::

## Why LinkedIn alone may not be enough

LinkedIn is powerful, but it is not designed for every introduction. Here are the common limitations professionals face.

### 1. It can be too broad

LinkedIn contains a lot of information. That is useful when someone wants to understand your full background, but not always ideal when you have only a few seconds to make an impression. At a networking event, client meeting or sales conversation, the other person may not need your entire career history — just who you are, what you do, and how to contact you. A focused digital identity makes this easier.

### 2. It keeps the relationship inside another platform

When you share your LinkedIn profile, the experience happens inside LinkedIn. That can be useful, but it also means the recipient is entering a platform full of distractions: notifications, feeds, other profiles, messages and content. A digital identity gives you a cleaner destination that presents exactly what you want someone to see first.

### 3. It is not always the smoothest offline sharing tool

If you meet someone at an event, you may say “Find me on LinkedIn.” Then the other person has to search your name, identify the correct profile, connect and possibly remember the conversation later. That process creates friction. A digital identity can be opened directly through an NFC tap, QR scan or link — the fewer steps involved, the more likely the connection is to continue.

### 4. It may not show the most relevant action

LinkedIn is built around profiles, content and networking. A professional digital identity can be built around action. Depending on your needs, your primary action may be:

- Save my contact
- Book a meeting
- Visit my website or view my services
- Contact me on WhatsApp
- Request a consultation

That focus can make a digital identity more effective during high-intent professional moments.

### 5. It does not always reflect your current business context

Many professionals have multiple dimensions to their identity. A founder may also be an advisor. A consultant may lead workshops. A real-estate professional may specialise in a specific property segment. LinkedIn may show the full person, but a digital identity can highlight the most relevant professional context for a particular audience.

## Why professionals still need LinkedIn

This does not mean LinkedIn is less important. In fact, LinkedIn remains one of the most valuable professional platforms. Professionals should continue using LinkedIn for:

- • Career credibility
- • Thought leadership
- • Search visibility
- • Hiring and recruitment
- • Industry networking
- • Public proof of expertise

::note
A digital identity should not fight LinkedIn. It should make LinkedIn easier to access when it is relevant. Your Linkist profile can include your LinkedIn profile as one of the key links — so someone can first understand your focused identity and then explore your broader professional background if they want to go deeper.
::

## When to use each one

**Use LinkedIn** when the goal is professional depth — when someone wants to review your career history, read your posts, see mutual connections, check recommendations or evaluate your credibility over time. If you are hiring, fundraising, applying for a role or building industry visibility, LinkedIn is extremely useful.

**Use a digital identity** when the goal is immediate clarity and action — when you are meeting someone at a business event, introducing yourself to a client, sharing your details through WhatsApp, adding a profile link to your email signature, presenting at a conference or sharing an NFC card.

::note
A digital identity works best when the recipient does not need to search, scroll or guess what to do next.
::

## What should your digital identity include?

A good digital identity should not include everything. It should include what helps someone understand and act.

### Essentials

- Name, role, company and professional headline
- Profile photo
- Mobile number or preferred contact method, and email
- LinkedIn profile and website
- One primary call to action

### Optional elements

- Services, portfolio and appointment link
- Company brochure or product page
- Social links and location
- Short introduction and contact-saving option

::note
Avoid crowding the page with too many links. A focused profile is usually stronger than a complete archive of everything you do.
::
