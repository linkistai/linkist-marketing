The plans answer four different questions, and the right one is the question you are asking this month. Prices below are in US dollars as listed on the pricing page; the store bills cards in AED and shows the currency at checkout.

## Essential: do you need a profile?

Free, for as long as you like. A digital business card with your photo, 1 personal profile, 1 template, sharing by QR code, link, email and SMS, 5 card scans a month, and import from your phone, a CSV or a VCF file. It is the plan for capturing the people you meet and keeping their context, with no card and no payment.

Every Linkist NFC card includes PRM Essential, so a card is the other way into it.

## Enhanced: do you need a better profile?

$2 a month, $12 a year or $25 for life. Enhanced is about the profile: a personal URL of the form linkist.ai/me/yourname, unlimited profile fields, bio, socials, services, products and certifications, 3 profiles (1 personal, 2 business), 3 card templates and a branded QR code. On the capture side it lifts card scans to 20 a month and adds a lead capture form and contact export.

Choose it if your profile is doing work for you: speaking, consulting, selling something under your own name.

## Pro: do you need relationship intelligence?

$10 a month or $100 a year, and the plan most people land on once the network is too big to hold in a head. Everything in Enhanced, 5 profiles with full template customisation and unlimited card scans, then the intelligence: AI Enrichment and lead scoring, ICP Matching and Network Ask, Intelligent Nudges and Top Actions, the AI voice notetaker and AI Follow-up, and a deal-tracking mini-CRM.

The AI features are optional and switched on with a separate consent; the [AI and your data](/ai) page explains what they read and how to switch them off.

::note
A rule of thumb: if you can name every relationship that matters and remember its context, Essential or Enhanced is enough. When you cannot, Pro is the plan whose features exist for that problem.
::

## Team: does the company need to keep its relationships?

$4 per user a month or $200 a year, minimum 5 users. Everything in Pro for every user, plus contact sharing across the team, a centralised admin console, company branding on cards and a team directory. Personal contacts stay with the person; team-shared contacts and their history stay with the company when someone leaves.

Enterprise is not a plan yet. It is interest only, with single sign-on, CRM and HRMS integration and product customisation planned for it.

## What a card adds

A card is one-time: Starter from AED 75 and Signature from AED 95, in PVC, cherry wood or brushed metal, each including PRM Essential. The Signature Bundle pairs any Signature card with a year of Pro for $100; the Founders Circle Bundle pairs the Founders Circle card with lifetime Pro for $150. Both cost less than the card and Pro bought separately; the [bundles page](/bundles) shows the arithmetic by material.

## Billing, plainly

Paid subscriptions renew until cancelled unless the checkout says otherwise, and a period already paid is not affected by a price change. Payments go through third-party providers such as Stripe; Linkist does not store full card numbers. Plans, AI credit top-ups, invoices and card orders are managed in the billing hub at prm.linkist.ai, with VAT shown upfront.

Compare every line on the [pricing page](/pricing), or start with Essential and decide later.
