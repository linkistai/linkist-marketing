Software that reads your contacts should be able to say, in plain words, what it does with them. This article does that for Linkist, using only what the company has published: the privacy policy and the terms of service, both version 1.1 dated 1 June 2026. Where the documents are silent, the article says so.

## The eight things it does

The privacy policy lists the optional AI features and what they produce. In the product's own names:

- **AI Enrichment** fills gaps in a saved contact from public or professional sources.
- **ICP Matching** compares your contacts with the profile of who you are looking for.
- **Relationship Priority** scores which relationships are active, cooling or cold.
- **Network Strength** reads the network as a whole.
- **Smart Signals** notice changes that deserve a reaction.
- **Warm Introductions** suggest who could introduce you, and to whom.
- **AI Follow-up** proposes the next action and drafts the message.
- **Contact summaries** condense notes, context and history into a short read.

## What it reads

Profile data, contact details, your notes, connection history, interaction context and professional information, plus public or professional sources for enrichment where it is enabled and lawful. That is the policy's list. It does not include anything outside Linkist about you, and it does not include your contacts' private data.

## The rules it runs under

- **Optional, with its own consent.** AI enablement is a separate consent. Contact enrichment, Smart Signals, ICP and Smart Introductions are named in it.
- **Assistive, never binding.** Outputs are suggestions you review. The policy says Linkist does not use AI for legally binding decisions; the terms say outputs may be inaccurate or out of date.
- **Bounded retention.** AI enrichment outputs are kept for up to 6 months or until you delete your account, unless refreshed, deleted or legally retained.
- **No sale, no marketing to your contacts.** Imported contact data is not sold, and the people in it are not marketed to unless they opt in themselves.
- **No minors.** Linkist is for people aged 18 or over and does not knowingly run AI profiling on minors.
- **Sensitive data stays out.** Health, financial, government-ID and biometric records must not be put into Linkist, and the AI must not be used to infer sensitive characteristics.

::note
Some AI actions draw on AI credits, sold as top-ups in the billing hub at prm.linkist.ai. What one credit buys is not published yet.
::

## The switch

Disable optional AI features, or object to automated profiling, in Privacy Settings in your account, or by writing to privacy@linkist.ai. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that already happened, and the rest of Linkist keeps working without the AI.

## What is not published

Which model provider processes AI requests, in which region, and the credit price list. The policy names the provider category and says some providers may be outside the UAE, with data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses among the safeguards. The [AI and your data](/ai) page keeps these as open items and cites every section above; the [security](/security) page does the same for hosting, retention and your rights.
