Software that reads your contacts should be able to say, in plain words, what it does with them. This article does that for Linkist, using only what the company has published: the Linkist Terms and Privacy, version 1.0, effective 7 September 2026. Where the document is silent, the article says so.

## The eight things it does

When you switch them on, the AI features may add public business information to your contacts, summarise, score and suggest (T 9, P 6). In the product's own names:

- **AI Enrichment** fills gaps in a saved contact from public business sources.
- **ICP Matching** compares your contacts with the profile of who you are looking for.
- **Relationship Heat Map** scores which relationships are active, cooling or cold.
- **Network Strength** reads the network as a whole.
- **Smart Signals** notice changes about a contact that deserve a reaction.
- **Warm Introductions** suggest who could introduce you, and to whom.
- **AI Follow-up** proposes the next action and drafts the message.
- **Contact summaries** condense notes, context and history into a short read.

## What it may use

Information from public business sources, from users, and from data providers Linkist has an agreement with; nothing else (T 9). Information about companies, such as a company's website or industry, may be reused for all Linkist users. Information about individual people stays in your account and is not shared with other users (T 9, P 6). Linkist does not let its AI providers use your data to train their own general AI models (T 9).

## The rules it runs under

- **Off until you switch it on.** AI features are off until you switch them on, you can switch them off at any time, and each optional use is asked for separately (T 9, P 5).
- **Suggestions, not decisions.** Each result shows how confident it is and why, and less certain results are saved only if you accept them. Linkist does not use AI to make decisions that seriously affect people (P 6). AI can be wrong, so check a suggestion before you rely on it (T 9).
- **Your message when you send it.** A message drafted by AI becomes your message when you send it (T 9).
- **Bounded retention.** AI results about people are kept for up to 6 months unless refreshed, or until you delete them (P 10).
- **No sale, no marketing to your contacts.** Linkist never sells personal data (P 8), and it sends no marketing or automatic Linkist invitations to your contacts (T 11).
- **Adults only.** Linkist is for people aged 18 and over (P 13).
- **Sensitive data stays out.** Health, bank account, ID document, religious or children's data must not be uploaded to Linkist (T 8).
- **Anyone can say no.** Anyone can ask Linkist not to add AI information about them (P 6, P 7).

::note
AI features use Credits (T 10). Credits and top-ups are handled in the billing hub at prm.linkist.ai.
::

## The switch

Switch the AI features off in Settings > Privacy in your account, or by writing to privacy@linkist.ai (P 5). You can use Linkist without them.

## What the document does not name

Which model provider processes AI requests, and in which region. The privacy part lists AI among the kinds of service provider Linkist uses and says providers may be in other countries, with protections such as data protection agreements and encryption when data moves (P 8, P 9). The [Terms and Privacy](/legal/terms) is the document every section above cites.
