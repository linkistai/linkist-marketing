The spreadsheet is the sign that something went wrong earlier. By the time you are typing names into a column called Follow up, the context is already thin and the timing is already late. The fix is not a better spreadsheet. It is a capture habit that takes 10 seconds at the stand and a routine that takes 20 minutes the next morning.

## At the stand: capture the person and the reason

Get the contact into Linkist while the conversation is still warm. There are 6 ways in, and the right one depends on what the other person has in their hand.

- They have a phone with NFC: tap your Linkist card. Your live profile opens on their screen, they save your details, and if they use Linkist too the meeting is captured on both sides.
- They have any phone: show your QR code. Same profile, same result.
- They give you a paper card: scan it with the camera. The name, company, role, phone and email land in fields you can correct before saving.
- They are already in your phone or in another tool: import from the address book, a CSV or a VCF file later.

Then the part the spreadsheet never holds. Add a tag for the event, note where you met, and record a voice note of one sentence: what they need, what you offered, what you promised. That sentence is the difference between a follow-up that lands and a generic message.

::note
Ten seconds of context beats ten minutes of recall. A note recorded at the stand is exact. The same note written three days later is a guess.
::

## The next morning: one pass, not a project

Open Linkist with coffee and go through yesterday's captures once.

1. Fill the gaps. On Pro and Team, AI Enrichment adds missing professional details to incomplete records. You see what was added and can change it; it is optional and switched on with its own consent.
2. Mark who fits. If you keep an ideal customer profile, ICP Matching shows which of yesterday's people match it. The home screen tells you how many matches were found.
3. Decide the first move for the people who matter. A reply, an introduction, a document you promised. Put it in the Weekly Planner so it is scheduled rather than remembered.

That is the whole pass. Twenty minutes for 30 contacts, because the context is already there.

## The week after: let the nudges carry it

This is where a spreadsheet dies, because nobody opens it on Thursday. Linkist does the opening for you.

- Top Actions lists the most important things to do today when you open the app.
- Intelligent Nudges prompt you before a follow-up slips: you met someone at the event and have not followed up.
- AI Follow-up drafts the message from the context you saved. You read it, change it, and send it yourself. Linkist never sends on your behalf.
- Relationship Priority shows which of the new relationships are warming up and which are going quiet, so the second week gets the right attention too.

## What to skip

Skip the mass email to everyone you met. It signals that you remember no one. Skip connecting on every platform at once. And skip the spreadsheet: if the context is in Linkist, the list is already sorted by what deserves attention.
