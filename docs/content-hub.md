## Feature captions (under 30 words each)

Capture:

- NFC tap and QR: Tap a Linkist card to a phone, or show your QR code. The other person gets your live profile and you keep the meeting.
- Card scan: Point the camera at a paper business card and it becomes a contact record. 5 a month on Essential, 20 on Enhanced, unlimited on Pro.
- Import and add: Bring your phone contacts, a CSV or a VCF file, or add a person by hand. Tags and a voice note keep the context with them.
- AI Enrichment: An incomplete record gets its missing professional details filled in. You see what was added and can change it.

Find:

- Natural-Language Search: Type what you remember: the fintech founder from GITEX, the designer who mentioned Riyadh. Linkist searches names, notes and context together.
- ICP Matching: Describe your ideal customer, partner or hire once. Linkist shows which contacts fit and how many strong matches you already have.
- Relationship Priority: Relationship Health shows who is warming up, cooling down and cold, so attention goes where it is needed.
- Network Ask: Post what you need. Linkist finds relevant people in your network, or a trusted path to someone one connection away.

Act:

- Top Actions: The most important things to do today, on the home screen, ordered by what deserves attention.
- Intelligent Nudges and Smart Signals: You met Julian at GITEX. Want to follow up? A nudge arrives before the moment passes, and signals tell you what changed.
- Weekly Planner: Plan the relationships and opportunities to work on this week, then tick them off.
- Warm Introductions and AI Follow-up: Ask for an introduction through someone you both know, and let Linkist draft the follow-up from the context you saved.

Profiles and cards:

- Digital business card: Photo, name, role, company and the links that matter. One template on Essential, 3 on Enhanced, full customisation on Pro.
- Personal URL: A memorable address of your own under linkist.ai/me. It is on your card, in your email signature and on the QR code.
- Several profiles: A personal profile and business profiles for the hats you wear: 1 on Essential, 3 on Enhanced, 5 on Pro. Switch which one a card shares.
- Sharing and wallet: Share by NFC tap, QR code, link, email or SMS, and add the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

Teams:

- Shared Contacts: Contacts and their context are shared with the authorised team, so a colleague can pick up a relationship with the history in place.
- Admin console: Add and remove users, manage who sees what, and keep company branding on every card from one place.
- Team directory: Every colleague and their profiles in one directory, so the right person can be introduced fast.

Four-word versions for tiles and ads:

- AI Enrichment: Turns incomplete contacts into richer records automatically.
- Natural-Language Search: Find people using what you remember, not exact names.
- ICP Matching: Tell Linkist who you are looking for and see who fits.
- Intelligent Nudges: See what changed and what deserves action today.
- Keeps the context: Know why the person mattered, not just their number.
- Finds the right people: Search, match and uncover opportunities inside the network you already have.
- Tells you what needs attention: See important relationships and actions before they slip through the cracks.
- Helps you act: Follow up, reconnect, or find a warm introduction with the context already in place.
