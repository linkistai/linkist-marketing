# Independent review: fresh-eyes QA pass

Date: 11 September 2026. Reviewer: independent (no part in the build). Target: the production build on http://localhost:3200. Method: Chromium (Playwright 1.62) on all 58 routes (the 47 sitemap routes, the 8 legal drafts, `/system`, a 404 probe) at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1360, 1440 and 1920, with axe-core at 390 and 1440; an interaction pass on the home page, an article, the forms, the menu, the motion states and the assistant; the rendered copy of the 12 required pages read in full and the rest skimmed; a source read of the components behind each interaction. Firefox could not be launched on this machine and WebKit cannot be assessed against plain-http localhost (see L13), so both engines remain unverified.

## Summary

26 findings: 2 high, 8 medium, 16 low. The site is in good shape where the automated bar sits: 0 axe violations on every route at both widths, no horizontal scroll at any of the 8 widths, one H1 and no heading skips on every static page, every internal link and anchor resolves, the legal pages are dated, no em dashes or unresolved markers outside the places the decisions allow, and the assistant, carousel, chips, currency switch, FAQ and forms behave. The two high findings are both layout regressions the brief names explicitly: from 1024 to 1440 the floating "Ask Linkist" launcher sits on top of the footer's "Motion" and "Cookie choices" controls, and the footer newsletter input collapses to 81 px at 1024 and 144 px up to 1440. The medium findings are a hidden H1 once the hero auto-advances, a motion switch that cannot be turned back on under `?motion=off`, a footer "Cookie choices" control that does nothing in this build, an assistant panel keyboard users cannot reach, a pricing page that contradicts itself on the card currency, sub-44 px targets throughout the footer, legacy legal URLs that 404, and inconsistent public DNS for the "Get NFC Card" host.

## Checked and found clean

- **Responsive, 8 widths x 58 routes (Chromium):** no horizontal scroll (`scrollWidth == clientWidth` at the top and the bottom of every page), and no visible element outside the viewport except inside intended scroll strips (stage flow, feature tabs, help categories, comparison table, preview row). The header pill fits at 360 (wordmark, "Get the App", menu). Phone frames keep their corners and island at 360 and 1440 (container units). Newsletter input at 768 is 271 px (the Appendix B tablet case passes).
- **axe-core (wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21a, wcag21aa, wcag22aa, best-practice) at 390 and 1440, motion off:** 0 violations on all 58 routes, including colour contrast on the dark theme; the "Most popular" and "Most chosen" badges are white pills with dark text, tag chips and plan tags pass.
- **Headings and landmarks:** exactly one H1, no level skips, one `main`, `lang="en-GB"` on every route in the static DOM (the hidden-H1 case above is the only exception and only after auto-advance). Footer column headings are H2s after the page sections.
- **Keyboard:** the first Tab lands on a visible "Skip to content" link; 70 Tab stops on the home page all show the coral focus outline and have names; carousel tabs use roving tabindex with ArrowLeft/ArrowRight/Home/End moving both selection and focus; FAQ summaries toggle with Space; both savings tables are focusable regions with names; the design-preview strip is focusable; the stage flow, feature tabs and help category strips contain focusable buttons; mobile menu opens with a labelled button, locks body scroll, closes on Escape.

Switching the footer switch off stops Lenis at once (a 600 px wheel scrolls 600 px instantly; with motion on it eases 49, 206, 567 px).
